There are a lot of opinions right now about how to build software with AI. These are ours — and the difference is: we don't argue with slides, we argue with [projects you can click](/projects/).

<!--more-->

## 1. We build instead of presenting.

This was on this website before the AI wave: we start where presentations fail. A feasibility study answers "will it work?" worse than a two-week prototype — and is rarely cheaper.

## 2. The AI does the typing. Not the thinking.

Agentic development doesn't mean handing responsibility to the machine. Every line passes review by an engineer with over twenty years of experience. The result is senior software in a fraction of the time — not fast software at junior quality.

## 3. Tools change monthly. Principles don't.

Calibrated trust, review discipline, small verifiable steps: these carry through every tooling cycle. Anyone selling you a fixed curriculum or a months-long transformation program is selling you a snapshot that's outdated by kickoff.

## 4. We demonstrate instead of promising.

That's why we sell [built experiments](/offers/innovationspartner/) instead of strategy phases, and [prototypes with an honest verdict](/offers/prototyp-sprint/) instead of feasibility studies. One promise we can always keep: at the end, there's something that runs.

## 5. "Don't build this" is a valid outcome.

A verdict that can never come back negative isn't one. If your use case doesn't hold up, we'll put that in writing — before you spend the big budget, not after.

## 6. Evidence is public and dated.

The [Village Generator](/projects/village/) and the [Oberheim Matrix 1000 editor](/projects/oberheim-matrix1000/) were built largely AI-assisted and sit on the open web with dates attached. We don't tell you how well we work with AI — you can check.

## 7. Hours are the wrong unit.

AI lowered our costs, not our value. So we sell outcomes at fixed prices — and carry the risk of our own speed ourselves. You can put a fixed price on your own work. You can't put one on other people's change.
