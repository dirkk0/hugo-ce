**AI has collapsed the cost of validation. If you still manage innovation with studies and stage gates, you're paying 2020 prices.**

<!--more-->

Innovation management used to mean workshops, trend reports, a feasibility study — and one big bet per year, because building anything cost six figures. That's over. An experiment now costs days, not months. Which makes innovation management empirical: **ideas don't get assessed anymore, they get built — and the weak ones die cheaply.**

## What you get

Per quarter:

1. **Three built experiments** — each one real, clickable software testing an innovation hypothesis from your organization. No slide deck.
2. **Three written verdicts** — continue, pivot, or bury, with reasoning. *"Don't build this"* is a valid and above all cheap outcome: that's exactly what experiments are for.
3. **A portfolio review** — what the quarter's experiments say about your roadmap, and which hypotheses come next.

The code is yours. Everything is documented and handover-ready.

## How it works

- **Monthly rhythm:** At the start of each month we pick the hypothesis together (half a day, remote). Then we build. By month's end, the experiment and its verdict are on the table.
- **Quarterly terms:** You renew per quarter — or you don't. No annual contracts, no lock-in.
- **Price: €24,900 (net) per quarter.** Pilot price for the first partners: **€19,900** — in exchange for a citable case study.
