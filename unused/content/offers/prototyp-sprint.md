**From AI idea to working prototype in two weeks — plus an honest verdict on whether it's worth building further.**

<!--more-->

Everyone tells you AI matters. Nobody tells you concretely whether *your* use case works, what it costs, and where it breaks. Consultancies sell you a strategy phase. Vendors sell you their product. We do something different: **we build the thing — and then tell you honestly what it's worth.**

## What you get

1. **A working prototype** of your use case — real software, clickable, running on your data (or realistic sample data), demoable to your management.
2. **A sober feasibility verdict** — ten pages max: what works, what doesn't, what productionizing would cost, where the risks are (including data protection and the EU AI Act). If the honest answer is *"don't build this,"* that's exactly what we'll write.
3. **A recorded handover** — prototype, verdict, and reasoning, presented to your team.

The code is yours. No lock-in, no obligation to continue.
