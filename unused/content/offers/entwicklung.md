**Custom software at a fixed price, in weeks not months — built by a senior with AI leverage instead of a junior team on timesheets.**

<!--more-->

Custom software used to mean: a six-figure quote, six months of runtime, agency overhead. That's why most good ideas for internal tools were never built. This has changed: what didn't get budget yesterday is worth commissioning today.

## Terms

- **Fixed price, fixed scope, fixed date.** No hourly billing. Typical range: **€8,000–45,000**, delivered in 1–6 weeks.
- **Senior-only:** every line passes review by an engineer with 20+ years of experience. The AI does the typing, not the thinking.
- **Production-grade by default:** tests, deployment, documentation, handover. Optional ongoing operations via a monthly care package.
- The code is yours.

No quote without a written one-page spec — for anything fuzzy, the [AI Prototype Sprint](/offers/prototyp-sprint/) or a scoping workshop is the right entry point. Change requests are welcome and transparently re-quoted.
