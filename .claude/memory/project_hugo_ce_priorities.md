Dirk's stated priority for the Curious Electric site, 2026-09-12:

> "To me it was important that the home page is error-less and somewhat SEO optimized.
> Other than that a very slow optimization over time would be ok."

**Why:** it is a one-person company's shop window, not a product. The home page is what a
prospective client or a search engine actually sees, so correctness and SEO there carry
real weight. Deep pages are long-tail and can improve at leisure.

**How to apply:**
- Treat a problem on the **home page** as worth fixing now. The same problem three levels
  deep is a candidate for [[queued-autonomous-work]], not an interruption.
- Do not propose site-wide sweeps, refactors or consistency passes as urgent work. Offer
  them for the queue and let him choose.
- Correctness and SEO on the home page beat visual polish or internal tidiness anywhere.
- This is a triage rule, not a ban: if something deeper is genuinely broken (a 404, a
  broken deploy, invalid markup that breaks rendering), say so.
